The project aims to optimize post-hoc rule-based counterfactual explanation algorithms within the LORE (Local Rule-Based Explanations) framework to make them more efficient and scalable for real-life usage. The solution will cater to diverse users such as developers  students  data scientists  and stakeholders  helping them understand and refine machine learning models. Furthermore  the project aligns with the goals of the FAIR Research Programme and PNRR  particularly in areas of transparency and trustworthy AI.
