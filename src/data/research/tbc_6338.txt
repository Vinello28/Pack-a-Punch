The Large Language Models (LLMs) era has introduced a series of new challenges in the field of Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HCAI). In particular  a widely spread framework known as Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) provides a methodology for interfacing users with a Knowledge Base (KB) through natural language. In fact  RAG is one of the most common ways to expand an LLM’s knowledge apart from Fine Tuning  which is the reason why this framework is currently so popular in the field. This context introduces multiple risks in terms of usability and security  mainly due to the problem of Hallucinations  where an LLM  when faced with a question  tends to  invent  information to formulate a plausible answer  especially when it lacks sufficient knowledge to provide a correct response. In this context  we introduce the HC-RAG platform  which aims to implement a platform that provides these requirements to the so-called  Human-in-the-loop . This will enable him to monitor the RAG system ¿ from the conversations viewpoint  monitoring the information received by the LLM at inference time and thus being able to intervene on retrieved data and the response itself
