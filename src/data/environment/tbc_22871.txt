this paper examines the up-to-dateness of installed firmware versions of iot
devices accessible via public internet. it analyzes datasets of 1.06m devices
collected from the iot search engine censys and maps the results against the
latest firmware version each manufacturer offers. by applying the semma data
mining process, a fully scalable and adaptive approach is developed. this
approach relies on three data artifacts: raw data from censys, a mapping table
with firmware versions and a keyword search list. the preliminary results
confirm the heterogeneity of connected iot devices. they show that
manufacturer, device type and country influence the up-to-dateness of firmware.
the results suggest users as a "weak link" as they do not update the firmware
of their devices in a timely manner. however, the heterogeneity leads to
results not showing a high reliability, yet.
