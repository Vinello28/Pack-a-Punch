poor air quality has become an increasingly critical challenge for many
metropolitan cities, which carries many catastrophicphysical and mental
consequences on human health and quality of life. however, accurately
monitoring and forecasting air qualityremains a highly challenging endeavour.
limited by geographically sparse data, traditional statistical models and newly
emergingdata-driven methods of air quality forecasting mainly focused on the
temporal correlation between the historical temporal datasets of airpollutants.
however, in reality, both distribution and dispersion of air pollutants are
highly location-dependant. in this paper, we proposea novel hybrid deep
learning model that combines convolutional neural networks (cnn) and long short
term memory (lstm)together to forecast air quality at high-resolution. our
model can utilize the spatial correlation characteristic of our air pollutant
datasetsto achieve higher forecasting accuracy than existing deep learning
models of air pollution forecast.
