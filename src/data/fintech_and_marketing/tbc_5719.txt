The project aims to develop an AI-based tool using Large Language Models (LLMs) to automate regulatory analysis in the financial sector  focusing on simplifying complex regulations  improving compliance quality  and enhancing process efficiency. The tool will support regulatory interpretation  consultation  and advisory tasks  enabling financial institutions to navigate and adhere to legal requirements more effectively. Key objectives include creating an AI model that provides accurate  transparent  and contextually relevant information  supported by human oversight to ensure ethical  legal  and reliable outputs. Additionally  the project is committed to environmental sustainability through optimised computational processes  digital dissemination  and sustainable data management practices.
