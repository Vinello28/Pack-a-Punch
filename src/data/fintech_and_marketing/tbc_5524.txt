this paper examines the relationship between inverse perpetual swap
contracts, a bitcoin derivative akin to futures and the margin funding interest
rates levied on bitmex. this paper proves the heteroskedastic nature of funding
rates and goes onto establish a causal relationship between the funding rates
and the bitcoin inverse perpetual swap contracts based on granger causality.
the paper further dwells into developing a predictive model for funding rates
using best-fitted garch models. implications of the results are presented, and
funding rates as a predictive tool for gauging the market trend is discussed.
