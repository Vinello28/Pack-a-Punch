SIMQuSEC focuses on the exploration of strongly interacting models  like high-spin Hamiltonians or multi-component bosonic systems  for the development of unconventional quantum error correction codes as well as on the use of ultracold gases as effective quantum simulators of strongly interacting quantum many-body systems and exotic states of matter. More specifically  it will analyse Quantum Double Models  also for continuous variables  as quantum computing paradigms able to provide unconventional topological models that go beyond the standard qubit formulation. In order to examine fault-tolerance  it will describe their mathematical and physical properties. Also  driven by recent experimental advancements in reconfigurable atom arrays  it will consider unconventional stabilizer codes  as well as higher-rate codes like Quantum Low-Density Parity-Check codes. At the same time  SIMQuSEC will develop the theoretical framework required to quantum-simulate certain classes of strongly interacting models. It will be interested in continuum models describing two-component systems made of bosons and fermions with strong pairing interactions between the two components.
