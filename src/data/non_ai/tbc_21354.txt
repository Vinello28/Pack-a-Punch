The project addresses the integration of quantum light sources into photonic platforms realized into commercially available LiNbO3 thin films  aligning with the dual objectives outlined in the A3 NQSTI call  namely the realization of (i) hybrid platforms that integrate deterministic single-photon sources and monolithic LiNbO3 platforms  and of (ii) nanoscale LiNbO3-based sources of entangled photons using spontaneous parametric down conversion in the telecommunication window
