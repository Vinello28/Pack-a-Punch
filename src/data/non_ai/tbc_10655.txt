Industrial networks are crucial for the transformation of manufacturing plants into smart factories. These  networks rely on heterogeneous technologies to provide ubiquitous communication  sensing  and  computing capabilities for a variety of use cases with diverse performance requirements. Nonetheless   industrial environments are impaired by heavy multipath propagation and frequent non-line-of-sight  conditions with negative effects on the system performance. Furthermore  industrial processes involve  several fixed and mobile assets that must be monitored and controlled.
