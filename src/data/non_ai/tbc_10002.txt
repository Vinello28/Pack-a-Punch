The REFERENCES project stands as a pioneering endeavor at the intersection of technological innovation and societal evolution exploring the frontiers of future networks and services within the RESTART initiative ecosystem. This multifaceted initiative delves into a spectrum of critical aspects  each contributing to the understanding of the complex landscape ahead. The project navigates the intricate web of challenges and opportunities to define our digital future  from the imperative of safeguarding e-privacy in an increasingly interconnected world to groundbreaking agreements shaping new services. It grapples with the regulatory nuances of net neutrality  developing an awareness of its impact and leading the creation of algorithms and protocols aligned with evolving regulatory frameworks. Furthermore  the project takes a hands-on approach  developing testbed systems that foster the evaluation of cutting-edge technologies within the dynamic realms of 5G and 6G. Beyond the technical intricacies  the REFERENCES project ascends to a 'high-level' perspective  scrutinizing the socio-cultural  political  legal  economic  environmental  and ethical implications that these emerging networks and services may entail. The REFERENCES project  does not aim at merely predict the future
