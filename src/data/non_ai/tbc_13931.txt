Pharmaceutical research is undergoing a transformative shift with the emergence of 3D cell models like spheroids and organoids. These structures offer unparalleled opportunities in understanding human physiology and disease mechanisms  promising revolutionary advancements in drug discovery and development. However  their adoption faces significant challenges due to the complexities of their three-dimensional architecture  resulting in high variability and inconsistent reproducibility. Efforts to mitigate these challenges through size-based standardization methods have shown promise but fall short. Innovative strategies are urgently needed to standardize 3D cell models  enhance reliability  and unlock their full potential for drug testing integration.
