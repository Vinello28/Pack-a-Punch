The project focuses on evaluating advanced flow cytometry multimodal techniques for cancer diagnosis. Circulating tumor cell (CTC) identification in microfluidic cytometry presents challenges in liquid biopsy for early cancer detection. A machine learning-powered tomographic phase imaging flow cytometry (TPI-FC) system can address this, producing high-throughput 3D phase-contrast tomograms. The core objective of this proposal is to elucidate distinctive signatures within label-free parameters that correlate with diverse tumor phenotypes, thereby delineating unique clinical scenarios.
