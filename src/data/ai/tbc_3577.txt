The project aims to use Artificial Intelligence to automate the programming and use of AGVs for intra-logistics in
manufacturing SMEs excluded from I4.0 innovation due to lack of resources, creating AGVs and IoT devices that
avoid the use of lines of code to re-program robots and machinery in order to adapt them to variable production and
layout by solving the following problems:
• Absence of programming/interfacing standards for industrial AGVs
• Shortage of qualified personnel
• Complexity of reprogramming the AGVs with each change in the production layout
The project will enhance the contents of the patent filed by Co-Robotics at the UIBM on 15/06/2018 and granted on
03/07/2020, with number 102018000006369 and title "MODULAR SYSTEM AND METHOD FOR CREATION
AND/OR PROTOTYPING OF SELF-CONFIGURING SYSTEMS”.
