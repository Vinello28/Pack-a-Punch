semantic parsing considers the task of mapping a natural language sentence into a target formal representation, where various sophisticated sequence-to-sequence (seq2seq) models have been applied with promising results. generally, these target representations follow a syntax formalism that limits permitted forms. however, it is neither easy nor flexible to explicitly integrate this syntax formalism into a neural seq2seq model. in this paper, we present a structure-aware self-attention language model to capture structural information of target representations and propose a knowledge distillation based approach to incorporating the target language model into a seq2seq model, where grammar rules, sketches or extra corpus are not required in the training process. an ablation study shows that the proposed language model can notably improve the performance of the baseline model. the experiments show that our method achieves new state-of-the-art performance among neural approaches on four semantic parsing (atis, geo) and python code generation (django, conala) tasks.
