some of the main challenges in skeleton-based action recognition systems are redundant and noisy pose transformations. earlier works in skeleton-based action recognition explored different approaches for filtering linear noise transformations, but neglect to address potential nonlinear transformations. in this paper, we present an unsupervised learning approach for estimating nonlinear noise transformations in pose estimates. our approach starts by decoupling linear and nonlinear noise transformations. while the
linear transformations are modelled explicitly the nonlinear transformations are learned from data. subsequently, we use an autoencoder with l 2 -norm reconstruction error and show that it indeed does capture nonlinear noise transformations, and recover a denoised pose estimate which in turn improves performance significantly. we validate our approach on a publicly available dataset, nw-ucla.
