At the core of the project is the development of an AI-based intelligent assistant, designed to process and interpret data in real time. This assistant will provide immediate alerts on potential hazards and offer operational guidance to the operator. The focus is on create an interaction between the AI system and the firefighter in the field, where the system will act as a real-time support tool. Also communication with other members of the team will be considered, i.e. enabling the team leader, not physically present at the site, to oversee operations remotely and provide more informed instructions.
