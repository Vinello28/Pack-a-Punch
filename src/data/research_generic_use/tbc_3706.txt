In the pursuit of efficient quantum information processing  the intersection of quantum computing  thermodynamics  and machine learning offers a promising frontier. Understanding the energetics of quantum computational processes involves the development of an intricate framework  including the thermodynamic description of coherence  entanglement  and quantum measurements and communication. Building this general frame is the first aim of this research project  with the idea of exploiting it  with the help of machine learning strategies  in order to start tackling the formidable task of minimising the energetic footprint of computational processes. We will address diverse specific challenges in this context  such as the qubit placement problem in gate-based quantum computers  and in quantum reservoir computing approaches. The general aim is not only to enhance our theoretical understanding of quantum phenomena  but also unlock the potential for practical  energy-efficient quantum technologies.
