early life

christopher foust's passion for the mysterious and the unknown began in his childhood, when he would often lose himself in tales of the eerie and unexplained. raised with a keen sense of curiosity, he developed an early fascination with stories that blurred the lines between reality and fantasy. whether reading about strange phenomena or exploring the depths of his own imagination, christopher’s youthful intrigue set the foundation for his future as both an author and an investigator.

from a young age, christopher’s interest in the uncanny was matched by a love for hands-on exploration and technical skills. he gravitated toward mechanics, electronics, and aviation, eventually becoming a licensed remote and private pilot, ham radio technician, and drone pilot instructor. alongside these technical pursuits, he nurtured his creative side through storytelling, capturing the eerie essence of his childhood wonder in his writing. these early experiences would later influence his works, including his simuology series and tales in the night, which blend his fascination with the unknown with a lifetime of diverse skills and knowledge.

raised in a family that valued both imagination and practicality, christopher developed a unique blend of curiosity, creativity, and technical expertise. today, he channels these influences into his work, sharing stories that spark a sense of mystery and wonder in those who seek to explore the hidden dimensions of the world around them. christopher lives in castle rock, colorado, with his wife, six children, and grandchild, continuing to craft tales that invite readers to venture into the realms of the eerie and unexplained.
