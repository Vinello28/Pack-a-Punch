modern vision-based reinforcement learning techniques often use convolutional
neural networks (cnn) as universal function approximators to choose which
action to take for a given visual input. until recently, cnns have been treated
like black-box functions, but this mindset is especially dangerous when used
for control in safety-critical settings. in this paper, we present our
extensions of cnn visualization algorithms to the domain of vision-based
reinforcement learning. we use a simulated drone environment as an example
scenario. these visualization algorithms are an important tool for behavior
introspection and provide insight into the qualities and flaws of trained
policies when interacting with the physical world. a video may be seen at
https://sites.google.com/view/drlvisual .
