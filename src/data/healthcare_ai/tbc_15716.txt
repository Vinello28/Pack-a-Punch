this article describes a contour-based 3d tongue deformation visualization
framework using b-mode ultrasound image sequences. a robust, automatic tracking
algorithm characterizes tongue motion via a contour, which is then used to
drive a generic 3d finite element model (fem). a novel contour-based 3d dynamic
modeling method is presented. modal reduction and modal warping techniques are
applied to model the deformation of the tongue physically and efficiently. this
work can be helpful in a variety of fields, such as speech production, silent
speech recognition, articulation training, speech disorder study, etc.
