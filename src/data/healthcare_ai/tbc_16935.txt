The objective of SHIFT-AI is to develop an Explainable AI model for the scheduling of the operating theatre and integrate it in the weekly workflow of AOU Parma (testing facility). The integration of AI in critical care presents a paramount potential  but also unique challenges on ethical implementation  as physicians often deal with life-threating conditions requiring rapid actions and patients unable to participate in the decisional process. Keeping human-in-the-loop is the leverage  to build trustworthy models and create human-machine interaction.
