this work summarizes our submission for the task 3: disease classification of
isic 2018 challenge in skin lesion analysis towards melanoma detection. we use
a novel deep neural network (dnn) ensemble architecture introduced by us that
can effectively classify skin lesions by using data-augmentation and bagging to
address paucity of data and prevent over-fitting. the ensemble is composed of
two dnn architectures: inception-v4 and inception-resnet-v2. the dnn
architectures are combined in to an ensemble by using a $1\times1$ convolution
for fusion in a meta-learning layer.
