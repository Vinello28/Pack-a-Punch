The project aims to develop  through the realisation of Proof of Concepts (POC)  a feasibility study for a software dedicated to the semi-automatic control of administrative regularity in PA  based on AI generative technologies. The study will examine various aspects  from technical feasibility to economic and operational feasibility  starting from the idea of creating a system that exploits generative AI technologies  which supports PA in the management of administrative regularity control processes in areas sensitive to maladministration  particularly in the public procurement sector.
