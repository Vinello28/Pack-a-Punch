The proposal aims to apply the research developed in EKSO on water pipelines (Smart Pipes) to create a use case and prototype  as well as a feasibility study  for validating and testing the technologies developed within Spoke 1  in the area of Human-centered Artificial Intelligence. This objective achieved through an operational platform for monitoring and managing groundwater in pilot site. The proposed solution utilizes decision support technologies for drought risk management  using  technologies and modules already in use at EKSO  and is supported by the development of AIVIP (Artificial Intelligence-based Validated Inspection for Pipelines). HUMAN-CENTERED AIVIP is an innovative  modular  configurable  and intelligent service designed to evaluate the life cycle of pipes in hydraulic networks.
