in this paper, we describe a telegram bot, mago della ghigliottina (ghigliottina wizard), able to solve la ghigliottina game (the guillotine), the final game of the italian tv quiz show l{'}eredit{\`a}. our system relies on linguistic resources and artificial intelligence and achieves better results than human players (and competitors of l{'}eredit{\`a} too). in addition to solving a game, mago della ghigliottina can also generate new game instances and challenge the users to match the solution.
